---
name: Session role policy
description: DJ and writer sharing behavior and permission boundaries between session roles.
---

DJs and Writers are distinct session roles and can share screen or audio using producer-style controls. These roles do not grant engineer-only recording or control permissions, or artist-only remote-control eligibility.

**Why:** DJs and Writers need recognizable ways to join and broadcast in sessions without broadening the existing control and recording privileges.

**How to apply:** Keep DJ and Writer identity in the shared role list and participant UI; grant broadcast behavior separately from engineer and artist authorization.
