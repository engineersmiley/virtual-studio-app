---
name: Session role policy
description: DJ sharing behavior and permission boundaries between session roles.
---

A DJ is a distinct session role and can share screen or audio using the producer-style controls. The DJ role does not grant engineer-only recording or control permissions, or artist-only remote-control eligibility.

**Why:** DJs need a recognizable way to join and broadcast in sessions without broadening the existing control and recording privileges.

**How to apply:** Keep DJ identity in the shared role list and participant UI; grant broadcast behavior separately from engineer and artist authorization.
